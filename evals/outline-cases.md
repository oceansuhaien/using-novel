# Outline Eval Cases

| Case | User prompt | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ideal | "Turn this scattered idea into a serial premise and volume 1 direction." | Produce premise, promise, protagonist engine, volume target, and risks. |
| Underspecified | "I want a cultivation story but do not know the hook." | Ask or propose limited hook options without freezing canon. |
| Conflict | "The summary says urban fantasy, but I now want post-apocalyptic academy." | Treat latest explicit direction as controlling if user confirms replacement. |
| Out of scope | "Make a full character relationship card." | Hand off to `novel-character-card-coach`. |
