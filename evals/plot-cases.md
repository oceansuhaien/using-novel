# Plot Eval Cases

| Case | User prompt | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ideal | "Design the hidden thread and reveal timing for the first volume." | Separate mainline, hidden thread, foreshadowing, and payoff. |
| Underspecified | "The arc feels weak." | Ask what phase goal, conflict, or payoff is currently weak. |
| Conflict | "Earlier notes say the mentor dies, but now I want the betrayal to reveal they survived." | Surface conflict and update if the new direction is confirmed. |
| Out of scope | "Build a setting bible for the sect system." | Hand off to `novel-outline-coach` or a future worldbuilding lane. |
